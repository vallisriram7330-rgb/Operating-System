Task 5 – Distributed and Cloud-Based Storage

Suggested design:
- NFS for simple campus-network file sharing.
- Ceph or HDFS for distributed storage where scale and replication are needed.
- Cloud object storage for remote access and backup.

Replication stores copies of data on multiple nodes to improve availability.
Strong consistency is appropriate for critical student-record updates.
Eventual consistency may be acceptable for cached or replicated media.

Device-management concepts:
- Device drivers: interface between OS and hardware.
- Buffering: temporary storage during data transfer.
- Caching: keeps frequently used data in faster memory.
- Spooling: queues jobs for devices such as printers.
- Block-device interface: transfers fixed-size storage blocks.
